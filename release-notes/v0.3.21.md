# Ether v0.3.21

Highlights:

- **Recommendations.** Home and every library's page are now designed in one place. A page is one list of rows, and each row says what fills it, which libraries it draws from, how it looks, and where, when and for whom it shows. Your current Home and collection rows move over on upgrade, and every app shows the result.
- **Search in one list.** Titles, franchises, people, seasons and episodes come back in one ranked list as you type, without waiting on TMDB, AI or a plugin's remote catalogue.
- **Plex apps that start without a decision play,** and changing subtitles or audio in Plex stays on the release you were watching.
- **Listening libraries** get lyrics, gapless playback, playlist import, likes and duplicate review, all as Ether capabilities any plugin library can use.

## Recommendations

**Recommendations** (under **Content** in the sidebar) replaces the Home layout designer, the library tab rows editor, the Home and library rows inside collections, and Discovery sources.

- **One full-width list of rows, which is the page itself.** Pick Home or any library's page from the toolbar at the top. The list is the order the page shows, with a **+** between rows to add one right there.
- **One kind of row.** Every row — including rows Ether suggests — has the same chips, drawer, drag handle and **Pin here** action. Opening and editing a row Ether placed saves it onto the page on the first change. Extra rows fold under **More rows you can add**, and **Why here** explains a row's placement in plain English.
- **Drag reorders and renumbers pins.** Drag any row (including by touch on phones and tablets) to move and pin it where it lands. Pins never collide: any pinned row displaced by a drop or edit automatically takes its new number, and pins survive switching between Curated and Personalized.
- **The row drawer** (fixed width across **Settings** and **Content**):
  - **Source:** Trending (Today or This week), New, Continue, Because you watched, a genre (including Anime), one of your collections, a plugin's shelf or list, Live now, and more.
  - **Libraries:** any number of them, not Movies, Shows or Both. On Home, choose one combined row or one row per library. "Trending" with Movies and TV Shows, one row per library, gives "Trending in Movies" and "Trending in TV Shows", and it works the same with ten libraries. "Any library" fan-out fills plugin libraries from their own shelves.
  - **Look:** one visual picker with sketches — Posters, Landscape or Hero (and Squares, Round, Tiles or Track list for listening libraries), with the usual look preselected — plus Numbered and Hide watched.
  - **Content:** a grid of every title the row would show right now, in its card shape, with ratings.
  - **Edit collection:** collection rows link straight to **Edit collection** in Collection Studio and return to the row with your draft intact.
- **Page settings drawer, with Settings and Tuning.** Open **Page settings** from the toolbar for two tabs: **Settings** (page mode, length, Home deduplication and app visibility) and **Tuning** (dials, Personal touches and How it learns).
- **Mode chosen once, per page:**
  - **Personalized** keeps your pinned and shown rows and lets Ether fill around them, up to a page length you set.
  - **Curated** shows only your rows in your order, keeping any pinned positions you set.
  - Each row is **Pinned** to a position, **Show**, **Auto** or **Hide**.
- **Schedules with global holidays.** A row can show on date ranges, evenings or weekends, or around a holiday. Holidays are worked out for every year: Christmas, Easter (Western and Orthodox), Hanukkah, Passover, Rosh Hashanah, Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn, Diwali, Holi, Vesak, Día de Muertos, Nowruz, Halloween, Thanksgiving (US and Canada) and the seasons.
- **Audiences and devices.** Show a row to everyone, only some viewers, or everyone except some, and on chosen device types.
- **Editing for and Preview as.** Edit a page for one viewer — including hiding or changing a row Ether placed, or adding a row — and the change stays in that viewer's variant without affecting anyone else. Previewing as a child shows their age limit and flags anything that would get past it.
- **Collections are content only.** Collection Studio no longer has an Audience tab. A new **Used on** tab lists every row that shows the collection, links to each one in Recommendations, and offers **Add to a page…** and **Browse in apps**. The gallery is one list of your collections plus Templates. Ether's built-in "Discovery source" collections are gone; their rows are built-in sources now.
- **Edit rows in library tabs.** A library's rows tab is called Recommendations, and **Edit rows** opens the designer on that library's page.
- **Plugin libraries** get real rows on their own pages and on Home: Continue listening, Liked, playlists, charts and each plugin's own shelves. Plugin lists can be placed as rows directly, and saved as a collection from the row drawer.

**Upgrading:** existing Home layouts, per-viewer Home changes, collection rows (with their schedules and audiences) and Discovery sources move into Recommendations automatically, once, the first time v0.3.21 starts.

**Every app honours it:**
- **Plex:** Home hubs and each library's Recommendations pivot follow the page.
- **Jellyfin and Emby:** Home sections come from the Home page. Numbered rows show at most 10.
- **Vela:** rows keep their look (Hero, Wide, Tiles, Track list), and plugin Home rows appear where you placed them.
- **Silo:** sections follow each row's look.
- **Stremio:** every library, including the built-in ones, has its own catalogue, and the Recommendations catalogue carries every Home watch row.

## Console

- **Overview:**
  - Fits a desktop window without page scroll: Server traces and the Activity chart share the spare height, Now playing, Health and the Activity table scroll inside their own panels, and Best rows shows as many whole rows as fit. Narrower screens stack and scroll as before, and plugin dashboard panels sit below the fold.
  - **Compact Health and header health pill:** healthy checks take one line each, with problems first and explained. One shared health pill appears in both Overview and the console header (replacing "All systems normal"), and clicking either opens the same **Server health** drawer with every item in full.
  - **Auto-refresh:** the title row, greeting and Refresh button are gone. Dashboard state and live streams update over the console stream, and 30-day activity and Best rows refresh quietly every 30 seconds while the tab is visible (keeping the last good figures if a refresh fails).
- **Sidebar regroup and Releases rename:**
  - The sidebar's **Server** group splits into **Connect** (Apps, Integrations, Plugins), **Automate** (Notifications, Flows) and **System** (General, Cluster, Maintenance), alongside **Content** and **Streaming**. Plugin pages declared for `server` appear under **System**.
  - **Quality** is renamed to **Releases** (`/releases`); typing `quality` in ⌘K still finds it.
- **Playback › Audio and Subtitles:**
  - **Audio & subtitles** is now two tabs — **Audio** and **Subtitles** — that edit the same household record and save together.
  - **Subtitles** covers subtitle behaviour only, split into **Settings** (languages, display, style and appearance) and **Timing fixes** (`/playback/subtitles/timing`).
  - **Subtitle providers** (OpenSubtitles, SubDL, SubSource, plus plugins and Stremio add-ons) moved to **Integrations** under a **Subtitle providers** group, with **Add subtitle provider** and **Check subtitles**. The tie-break provider order control is removed.
- **Support recordings include "Release search":** captures now include a **Release search** section in `REPORT.md` (and `release-search.json`) for plays caused by the recorded device, showing indexer queries and result counts, up to 40 checked releases (near misses first), rejection counts with examples, outcomes, and links to cached "not available" searches. Indexers are anonymised as "indexer A/B" with their kind — names, hosts and keys never leave the server — and titles, queries and release names follow the titles opt-in.
- Leaving a poster provider tab no longer takes the console down (#784, thanks @theatarr).

## Playback and Plex

- **Plex apps that start a stream without asking for a decision first** now play. Ether answered them with an error since 0.3.5; PMS does not require the decision.
- **Changing subtitles or audio in Plex** restarts on the same release instead of re-ranking and landing on a different one.
- **Audio and subtitle choice:** commentary and audio description never play while the file has a main track, a viewer whose languages a file lacks gets the original over a dub, and the first play of a new release honours your subtitle rules in Plex, Jellyfin Web and Emby (#782, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).
- **MP4 copies** rebuild empty H.264 and HEVC headers from the stream itself, so copies that used to fail now play (#783, thanks @oleedv).
- **Remux and Usenet playback:**
  - A paused copy no longer stays paused because an older segment request is still open (#778).
  - Play no longer hangs for a minute, then fails for up to an hour, after a Usenet warm-up fails (#779).
  - Progress is saved when a resumed MKV copy is served from its warm resume session (#780).
  - Thanks @megamikemann.
- **Trailers no longer mark films watched.** Opening a title whose trailer played to the end counted the film as finished and added "Because you watched". A one-time repair removes those plays.
- **Plex:**
  - When a new keystroke supersedes a Plex search, Ether now returns the endpoint’s usual empty results, including all 17 hubs for `/hubs/search`. A Roku app exit during search is still under investigation.
  - Catalogue pages honour the signed-in viewer's own library rules, not the household's (#800, thanks @theatarr).
  - Titles name the library that lists them, so two libraries split from one source keep their own titles in search, history and Continue Watching. Emby and Jellyfin are fixed the same way (#788, thanks @machetie).
  - Plugin library titles match Plex's album, artist and track types, and an album's first play waits as "preparing" instead of failing (#777, #781, thanks @machetie).
  - Ranked Top 10 rows no longer show blank cards in Plex Web and the desktop apps.
  - "Use Plex metadata" works again (#773, thanks @jmiller0).
  - Artwork links that name the server with its default port load (#801, thanks @kraigeisenman).
  - TV theme music comes from the same theme service PMS uses, on the first open, with YouTube only as a fallback.
- **Play Version menu:** WEB releases are listed again for 4K profiles. The menu is planned by priority, and the `resolver.sourceVersions` setting marks each source (Remux, Blu-ray, WEB) at 2160p, 1080p and 720p as Required, Offered or Off.
- **Infuse:** Jellyfin library rows list every version, so Infuse shows its version button. "Update Metadata" in Infuse, and Refresh metadata in any app, answers like the real servers.
- **Release matching:**
  - "Sxx Part 2" releases match (#796).
  - Season-range packs (`Season 1-5`) pick the episode from the right season folder (#798).
  - Play, the version picker and refills share one search cache entry per title (#797).
  - Korean broadcast shows and other titles with a presenter's possessive credit (such as a Hangul `의` credit or a named `"Presenter's ..."` prefix) match releases that omit the credit, while keeping strict matching when a possessive is part of the title itself.
  - Thanks @theatarr.
- **Debrid:** a rate limit from the playback CDN pauses background analysis only, not Plays on the account (#790). Offcloud and Debrid-Link now scope copyright refusals to the affected release. Other releases on the account can still play (#785). Thanks @theatarr.
- **Usenet background checks** count the bytes they actually moved against the daily allowance, so the default no longer stops after three checks (#791, thanks @theatarr).
- **Distributed playback** keeps serving segments once its recovery buffer is full (#789, thanks @theatarr).
- **Year in Review** counts the whole year for active viewers (#799, thanks @theatarr).
- **Per-app library visibility** is enforced on every playback route, not only in browsing.

## Apps

- **Jellyfin apps on Android** (Wholphin, Findroid, Fladder and others):
  - A copy is offered as the original alone, so the player no longer starts at 360p.
  - Apps that ask for subtitles in the stream get them as HLS tracks.
  - Switching subtitles retries instead of failing with a 404.
  - Findroid's Home no longer fails on the Collections and Live TV folders.
- **Jellyfin progress** sent without a play session keeps the stream alive (#803), and an actor's page lists their own work (#802), thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2.
- **Silo:** played titles open their detail page again, and turning off a burned-in subtitle stops burning it.
- **Credential-less streams** are checked against the device's own limits (#786, thanks @theatarr).

## Search

- **One ranked list** in every app: titles, franchises ("Mission Impossible" finds every film), people, seasons and episodes, with case and accents ignored.
- **Fast while typing:** title search never waits on TMDB, AI or plugins. Each new keystroke cancels the search before it.
- **Descriptions** ("cosy 90s heist films") use the Collections AI only when AI is set up and nothing named a title.
- **Plugin results** come after well-known titles that match, and a named artist's albums come right after the artist.

## Live TV

- **Ad breaks and program changes** on free streaming channels splice into the stream instead of showing slate and re-encoding.
- **Sturdier streams:** restarts follow a provider's stream-ID changes, channels with separate audio keep their first segment, encrypted audio is renumbered correctly, and browsers play copied channels with their audio.
- **Plex for iOS and Apple TV** show Watch on a programme that's on now, and Plex search puts the matching channel first (#764, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Multiview** keeps every tile at the live edge when quality changes.
- Closing a channel always stops its background work (#794, #795, thanks @theatarr).

## Vela

Update Vela to **0.2.6x or later** for:
- Recommendations rows and looks;
- one-list search;
- plugin screens and overlays;
- lyrics, gapless playback and crossfade;
- intro and credits skip markers;
- seek thumbnails.

Also:
- **Stable row keys:** feed rows now carry an opaque `key` that stays stable across rebuilds, and `/rows/{id}` consistently answers any superseded row snapshot with `409 cursor_expired` instead of `404`.
- **Skip markers in playback plans:** playback plans carry cached intro and credits skip markers (from chapters, the marker cache and plugin results, refreshed when switching versions) without delaying playback start.
- **Cross-origin Vela API and media reads:** `/vela/v0/*` and `/sessions/media/*` answer cross-origin requests (using bearer tokens and URL grants, never cookies; the owner console API stays same-origin), so Vela in a browser at a plain `http://` LAN address can probe HTTPS and no longer falls back to Plex.
- **Watch** in the console opens Vela at the address you are using, behind proxies and tunnels.
- Sign-in, refresh and playback retries never leave Vela stuck on a "conflict" error (#775, thanks @theatarr).

## Plugins and the SDK

- **Plugin screens:** plugins can describe document-based screens and surfaces (player overlays, toasts, Home rows and title panels) that Vela draws. Studio previews a draft's screens live, and you choose per app whether each one shows. Plugin images reach apps only through signed Ether links. The v0.3.19 section-based screen format (`sections`) has been removed; plugins using it must update to document screens.
- **Frontend adapters** can now serve a whole app protocol:
  - per-viewer app logins, where the plugin never sees the password;
  - Ether serves the media and artwork after its own access checks;
  - read and write each viewer's favourites, watch state and playlists;
  - browser access and form posts;
  - absolute stream links from the address the app used (#772);
  - open and search items like an Ether app;
  - their own logo on Apps.
- **Apps** shows proper marks for DLNA, WebDAV, M3U, Xtream and OpenSubsonic.
- **Source plugins** can opt in to category searches and typed lookups, for example Music and Books (#792, thanks @machetie).
- **SDK additions:**
  - `kinds.browse` takes several any-of groups per field (#767);
  - the undo journal is bounded (#766);
  - long field values work on PostgreSQL (#768);
  - `http.fetchToCache` makes room for resumable downloads (#770);
  - `multiSelect` settings draw as chips with search (#771), and `orderedList` settings as a drag-to-order list;
  - declared shelves name their purpose and layout.
  - Thanks @FizzWhirl.
- **List sync size** is declared by the plugin, shown in the install review and adjustable under the plugin's limits. Catalogue sources sync right after install.
- A plugin installed from a file updates from the Marketplace once it's listed there.

## Listening libraries

These are Ether capabilities. Any plugin library can use them.

- **Library experiences:** a library declares watch, listen, read, gallery or live, and every app draws it that way.
- **Lyrics** from the library's plugin, with word timing, translation and romanisation lines, served to every app by Ether.
- **Gapless playback** with a queued next track, and a crossfade handover of up to 15 seconds.
- **Loudness** is measured once a track is played through, and audio quality is reported to apps.
- **Playlists:** import from a link or an M3U or CSV file (matched by ISRC, then artist and title), with descriptions and the date each entry was added. Playlists and charts keep their own order in every app.
- **Likes** for tracks, albums and artists. Recently played knows what you played from, and a listening recap powers Rewind.
- **Duplicate review:** see every recording with more than one release and pick your preferred one.
- **Radio:** similar items and continuation for autoplay. An artist always opens with their full discography.

## Database performance

- **Catalogue background writes** (detail batches, season rows, summaries, recommendations, tally folds, facet naming and sync) now size batches by their measured lock hold, aiming for ~150 ms instead of rolling back fixed 32-title groups after 2 seconds. Recommendations commit in their own transactions and a lone title is never rolled back, eliminating multi-second SQLite writer stalls on small hosts (#804).
- **Plugin facet recomputes** during writes now pick their query plan by value share and bound their sort-index walk (falling back to the exact index-first read on a miss), preventing write timeouts on large PostgreSQL plugin libraries (#805).
- Plugin item updates, Home receipts, Live TV imports, planner statistics and maintenance no longer hold the database writer for seconds.
- Plugin storage quotas use a running total, and plugin tallies repair in place instead of rebuilding music libraries at every start.
- Unrestricted viewers' Home bootstraps no longer invalidate on catalogue, guide and favourite writes, and the Live now row reads the guide in batches instead of one query per channel.
- Vela retries and Plex row admission no longer scan or recheck every title (#793, thanks @theatarr).

## Upgrade notes

- **Recommendations migration:** the first start of v0.3.21 moves your Home layout, per-viewer changes, collection rows and Discovery sources into Recommendations, once. Collections keep their titles. Who saw a collection's row moves onto that row, and every viewer can browse collections in apps, subject to their age and library limits.
- **Console sidebar and routes:**
  - **Quality** is now **Releases** at `/releases` (`/quality` is no longer a route, though `quality` still matches in ⌘K search).
  - The sidebar's **Server** group split into **Connect** (Apps, Integrations, Plugins), **Automate** (Notifications, Flows) and **System** (General, Cluster, Maintenance).
  - **Playback › Audio & subtitles** split into **Audio** (`/playback/audio`) and **Subtitles** (`/playback/subtitles`, with **Timing fixes** at `/playback/subtitles/timing`), and **Subtitle providers** moved to **Integrations**.
- **Jellyfin and Emby:** the "Which rows" and "Personalized rows" settings under Apps are removed, because the rows now come from Home in Recommendations. **Home rows shown** stays.
- **Plugin screens:** plugins still declaring the v0.3.19 section-based screen format (`sections`) are refused until updated to document screens.
- **Plex apps that start a stream without a decision** now work; nothing needs changing.
- **Vela 0.2.6x** or later is recommended.
- A websocket library was updated for a published security advisory.
