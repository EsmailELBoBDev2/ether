# Ether v0.3.19

Highlights:

- **Sports.** A new Sports plugin brings live scores, standings and schedules for 17 leagues: NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, MLS, the Premier League, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, the Champions League, NCAA football and basketball, WNBA, F1, UFC and PGA.
  - **In Vela:** a Sports screen with box scores, play-by-play and win probability.
  - **Watching games:** each game shows where it is on your own IPTV channels, so one tap starts watching, with a live score bug over the stream.
  - **Favourites and spoilers:** favourite teams get close-game alerts, and spoiler-free mode hides scores.
  - **Data and cost:** it uses ESPN's public data, needs no account, and does nothing while nobody is watching.
- **Multiview.** Watch up to four Live TV channels at once in Vela. A multiview group counts as one stream toward a viewer's limit. Tiles start together, keep their live position when you change quality, and release their IPTV connection about 30 seconds after you leave.
- **Concerts and fuller music libraries.** Plugin libraries can now have branching levels, so a music artist can hold both albums and concerts, each with its own tab, shelves and card shape. Concerts play as video in every app.
  - Opening an artist or album fetches its full discography or tracklist straight away, so it's complete the first time.
  - Counts always match what is shown.
- **Download limits and per-viewer controls.**
  - Limit downloads by titles or GB, per day or per month, server-wide or per viewer.
  - Cap a viewer's download quality.
  - Stop converting video for one viewer's playback or downloads, separately.
  - Choose per viewer whether they may download at all.
  - Thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2 (#739, #747).
- **Portuguese and other regional indexers work.** Indexers that serve their API at `/api/newznab` (such as Netuno) now work from their plain address, and the discovered path is kept across restarts. An indexer that returns nothing for the standard Movies (2000) and TV (5000) categories is detected automatically, and Ether then uses its sub-categories or no category instead. Brazilian release names are read properly: `PT-BR`, `PTBR`, `Dublado`, `Legendado`, `Dual Áudio`. Portuguese dubs are no longer rejected for viewers who want Portuguese.
- **Flows redesign.** Flows opens on an Overview with charts that fit the screen (runs over time, top flows and triggers, average run time, recent failures). The Flows table has its own tab, and editing opens in a large drawer over the list. Seven bugs that stopped flows from saving are fixed, including the list going blank, durations in days, and hidden leftover settings.

## Playback

- **Infuse plays directly again:**
  - Infuse no longer gets a transcode offer it can't use, then a 404 on its direct stream.
  - A source id that has gone stale maps to the current release.
  - Infuse's "Play" answers in a fraction of the time.
- **Plex Web on titles with many subtitle tracks:**
  - Subtitle extraction no longer blocks playback, so audio no longer stalls every few segments.
  - Copied video and converted audio advance together.
  - The converter can no longer race far ahead and fill the disk.
  - Resuming starts a few seconds sooner (#740, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Plex for LG and other smart TVs:** the audio track menu lists every track, and text subtitles render in the format the TV asks for (#728, thanks @EmilioVelazquez).
- **Cast and crew on Plex for Apple TV, iOS and Google TV,** not just Plex Web. Plex library pages also make far fewer database queries.
- **Android apps:**
  - Streamyfin no longer restarts the stream on the first scrub, and chapters no longer disappear after a resume.
  - Silo on tablets resumes with video and keeps TrueHD and DTS when the tablet can decode them.
  - Thanks wapkaplet.
- **Ordered audio languages:** a viewer who lists German, then English, gets German releases first and English as the fallback. A bare `DUAL` no longer counts as any language you want (#743, thanks @kelzama).
- **Skip intro on Jellyfin and Emby:** playback fetches TheIntroDB markers for the title being played, so Skip intro appears (#744, thanks @kelzama).
- **Season packs:** each episode of a pack shows its own size, duration and tracks (#714). Plezy can optionally check offered versions at the same time as Auto (#715), thanks @alcroito.
- **Korean shows** find their releases again.
- **Upload budget:** a viewer's own playback rate is no longer mistaken for the server's upload capacity (#721, thanks @alcroito).
- **Optional throttle buffer:** set how far ahead of the viewer Ether prepares video (Playback › Converting for the app › More options). 0 keeps today's behaviour (#737, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).
- **Keep a resume point after N seconds,** instead of 5% of the title (General). 0 keeps the default (#748, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).
- **TorBox queued downloads:** when TorBox queues a torrent instead of starting it, Ether follows it until it starts and says "queued at TorBox behind N active downloads" (#746, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Usenet archives that can't stream** (compressed, first volume missing) are found by the check instead of failing on first play, and volumes posted out of order are put back in order (#726, thanks @FizzWhirl).

## Live TV

- **Smoother IPTV:**
  - Channels are copied instead of re-encoded whenever the player supports them.
  - Plex Web gets DASH straight from the copy.
  - VAAPI live encodes work.
  - A live stream no longer restarts underneath the player.
  - Encoders stop promptly when you stop watching (#731, thanks @rblair2).
- **Drag and drop** to reorder channels, with keyboard and touch support (#732, thanks @rblair2).
- **Live TV in Activity › History,** with the channel name and logo (#741, thanks @drondeseries).
- **Plex search finds Live TV channels** in Plex for iOS and Android TV (#725, thanks @kraigeisenman).

## Libraries and apps

- **Hide rules on built-in Movies and TV Shows,** for example keeping anime out of TV Shows (#719, thanks @alcroito).
- **Rules editor value picker:** pick a plugin field's values from chips or a search instead of typing them, combine them as Any of, All of or None of, and compare numbers and dates (less than, more than, between) (#749, thanks @FizzWhirl).
- **Jellyfin API keys and user admin:** create and revoke API keys from Jellyfin Web's dashboard, so Wizarr, Jellyseerr and migration scripts can create viewers, set their libraries and passwords, and pause them (#738, thanks @lordbeavis).
- **Sign-in lockouts:**
  - Viewers lists any active sign-in lockouts, with Clear and Clear all.
  - A successful sign-in clears that account's lockout.
  - Jellyfin apps get 6-digit Quick Connect codes, the format Jellyfin's own field accepts (#745, thanks @kelzama).
- **Pause a Plex friend** like any other viewer (#713, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Jellyfin collections show their artwork** in grids (#742, thanks @kelzama).
- **Plex:**
  - Discovery artwork uses PMS-style paths that every client accepts (#718).
  - Accounts use the same id everywhere, so monitoring tools match sessions to accounts (#720).
  - Batched metadata requests are fast again (#716).
  - Thanks @alcroito.
- **Plex viewer import:**
  - It's grouped by Plex.tv relationship.
  - It skips inactive and email-less accounts.
  - It backs off when plex.tv rate-limits.
  - Thanks @drondeseries (#729).
- **Jellyfin web player:**
  - It honours the quality and subtitle you pick, plus each viewer's track defaults, sorting and chapters.
  - You can copy settings from one viewer to another.
  - Thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2 (#723, #727).
- **Local network detection:** the source IP of a signed-in app decides whether it counts as local, in every app (#724, thanks @Seji64).
- **App compatibility rules** match a device by its name as well (#722, thanks @alcroito).
- **Faster pages:** Vela Home builds with far fewer database queries. Large plugin libraries open their people and collection tabs in tens of milliseconds instead of timing out, and their rows page went from 1,209 queries to 33.

## Plugins

- **Plugin search and sources:**
  - `indexer_search` can stream results as each indexer answers, report which indexers are still pending, and list the owner's indexers.
  - By default it stops in time to leave a plugin room to finish, and it skips indexers that keep stalling (#733).
  - Plugin sources open two at a time, like Ether's own releases, and equally good sources are ordered by measured speed (#734, #736).
  - Jobs can run Usenet checks within a daily allowance you can change (#735).
  - Long downloads can resume in `http.fetchToCache` (#730).
  - `media.probe` checks a file's running time (#712).
  - Thanks @FizzWhirl.
- **Branching levels, tabs per level and opening containers on demand,** for music, books and any plugin with nested items.
- **Plugin screens, live events and favourites** for plugins such as Sports, and an Overview tab for every plugin.
- **Restore:** a restore skips columns specific to the source server, and a cluster's backup imports on a standalone server (#717, thanks @XenoUniv3rse).

## Console

- **Every page names the browser tab** (`Page · Sub-tab — Server name`).
- **Server address** (main port, local, remote and Tailscale addresses, remote console) moved from Apps to General, below What viewers see.
- **Subtitle providers** lists SubDL and SubSource as well as OpenSubtitles, so all three can be reordered. Every provider is searched each time, and the order only breaks ties (#680, thanks @MBProMovies).
- **Trailers are on by default:** trailers and extras turn on whenever a Plex account is linked or `yt-dlp` is available. Upgrading switches them on once, and turning them off later under **Playback › Pre-rolls** is respected.
