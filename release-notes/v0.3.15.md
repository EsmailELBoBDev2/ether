# Ether v0.3.15

## Playback

- **Episodes released early on streaming services show up.** TMDB dates episodes by their TV broadcast, so a series a streaming service released all at once stayed hidden in Ether until each episode's broadcast date (Cunk on Cinema showed one of three episodes for a week). Once a season has started, opening or playing the show now checks for the rest of the season in the background. An episode appears as soon as a working release of it is confirmed (cached on your debrid service, or passing a health check), whatever TMDB's date says. Ether never shows an episode without a working release, keeps TMDB's numbering, and still turns down a different cut whose length doesn't match the episode. Each season is checked at most every six hours.

## Console

- **Descriptive search waits long enough for the AI.** A search like "funny space movies" now waits up to 20 seconds for the AI, up from 10. Typical models answer a new descriptive query in 12–15 seconds, so the first search often showed only title matches. Title searches stay instant, and a repeated descriptive search answers from cache.
- **Editing an IPTV source while it refreshes works.** Saving changes to a source, such as its groups or a new guide file, while the same source was refreshing failed at once with "Couldn't import this source". A save now waits for the refresh to finish. When a source can't be imported, the console shows Ether's actual reason instead of a generic "Check the source settings".

## Live TV

- **Channel logos show in Plezy on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.** Ether gave Plezy the logo address meant for Plex's own Apple apps, which Plezy can't load, so every logo was blank. Plezy now gets the same logos as on Android and the web.
- **Images, video and audio upload to the On Air library.** Every upload from a style designer, a reel or the Library was refused by Ether's request check (the upload left out a required header), so the progress bar stopped partway and the file never arrived. This affected every release since v0.3.5. A failed upload now also says why, and retrying the same file gets its own progress bar. PNGs whose metadata mentions SVG (common in images exported from design tools) upload as the PNGs they are, and an SVG Ether can't draw safely says to upload it as a PNG or JPG instead.
